How It Works

An inside look at how QShield dissects QR codes, extracts payloads, and runs static security heuristics.

1. Image Preprocessing & QR Decoding

When you provide a QR image, QShield validates the file structure and applies OpenCV image enhancement (contrast adjustment and adaptive binarization). The matrix is decoded into its raw text payload without rendering or navigating to it.

2. Payload Schema Classification

The decoded text is classified into its protocol schema: Web URL (HTTP/HTTPS), Wi-Fi configuration (WIFI:), Contact Card (vCard/MeCard), Payment Intent (UPI/SEPA), or Plain Text.

3. Static URL Heuristics

If the payload is a URL, it is decomposed into Scheme, Fully Qualified Domain Name (FQDN), Port, Path, and Parameters. QShield checks for suspicious characteristics: IP addresses, Punycode/IDN homograph characters, excessive subdomain nesting, high-abuse TLDs, and misleading credentials.

4. Explainable Scoring & Risk Bands

Each triggered indicator contributes points to a 0–100 risk score mapped to clear bands: Lower Detected Risk, Suspicious, High Risk, or Inconclusive. Detailed reasons and safety advice are provided directly to the user.
