Project Information

About QShield

An educational cybersecurity initiative focused on QR payload verification and quishing awareness.

Objective & Origin

As QR codes proliferated in daily transactions, ticketing, and physical authentication, attackers developed quishing attacks that bypass traditional perimeter defenses. QShield was engineered as a transparent, explainable platform to empower individuals and organizations to dissect QR codes before interaction.

Core Technology Stack

• Backend: Python with Flask & Werkzeug
• Computer Vision: OpenCV & Pillow for image decoding
• Static Heuristics: Custom Python URL parser with tldextract and dnspython
• Frontend: Semantic HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties, Vanilla JavaScript
• Database: SQLite3 with privacy-focused data scrubbing

Guiding Security Principles

1. Explainability over Obscurity: Scores are transparently decomposed into triggered indicators.

2. Zero Automatic Navigation: URLs are treated strictly as text data; never rendered or auto-opened.

3. Responsible Threat Defense: Low risk scores reflect heuristic absence of known malicious patterns, not an absolute guarantee of website safety.
