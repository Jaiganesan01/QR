# QShield Heuristic Risk Scoring Documentation

## 1. Overview & Core Tenets

QShield's risk scoring engine converts static, structural indicators identified during URL analysis into an explainable, deterministic score from **0 to 100**, categorized into four distinct risk bands.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Heuristic Assessment Notice:**  
> This score is **NOT** a statistically calibrated probability of phishing, nor is it generated by an opaque artificial intelligence or machine learning black box. It is a deterministic engineering heuristic based purely on observable lexical, syntactic, and structural patterns.

---

## 2. Indicator Weights

Weights are assigned based on how strongly an indicator correlates with evasion or deceptive tactics:

| Severity Level | Applied Base Points | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Info** | `0` | Informational disclosures (e.g. `URL_HTTPS_PRESENT_NOTICE`). Provides educational context without altering risk. |
| **Low** | `5` | Weak contextual signals (e.g., `URL_CREDENTIAL_KEYWORDS_IN_PATH`, `URL_URGENCY_KEYWORDS_IN_PATH`, `URL_EXCESSIVE_LENGTH`). Common on legitimate sites as well. |
| **Medium** | `15` | Notable evasion or structural anomalies (e.g., `URL_SHORTENER_DETECTED`, `URL_PUNYCODE_HOMOGRAPH`, `URL_EXCESSIVE_SUBDOMAINS`, `URL_UNENCRYPTED_HTTP`, `URL_NON_STANDARD_PORT`). |
| **High** | `25` | Strong deceptive techniques (e.g., `URL_USERINFO_PRESENT`, `URL_RAW_IP_HOST`, `URL_BRAND_IN_SUBDOMAIN`). |

---

## 3. Score Aggregation Strategy

To prevent artificial score inflation from correlated findings:

1. **Exact Deduplication:** If an identical indicator ID appears multiple times, it is evaluated exactly once.
2. **Correlated Indicator Discounting:** Related indicators are grouped into families:
   * `brand_mimicry`: (`URL_BRAND_IN_SUBDOMAIN`, `URL_BRAND_TYPOSQUAT_SUSPICION`)
   * `credential_context`: (`URL_CREDENTIAL_KEYWORDS_IN_PATH`, `URL_URGENCY_KEYWORDS_IN_PATH`)
   * `length_obfuscation`: (`URL_EXCESSIVE_LENGTH`, `URL_EXCESSIVE_QUERY_PARAMS`)
   * *Rule:* The first triggered indicator in a family receives 100% weight. Any subsequent indicator in the same family receives a **50% discount** (e.g., a secondary Medium indicator contributes +8 points instead of +15).
3. **Capping Boundary:** The final aggregate score is strictly clamped between `0` and `100`.

---

## 4. Risk Categories & Thresholds

| Score Range | Category | Meaning & User Guidance |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **0 – 19** | **Low** | Few or no suspicious indicators detected by configured rules. The URL exhibits standard formatting. |
| **20 – 49** | **Medium** | Some contextual or structural indicators warrant caution (e.g., shortened link, unencrypted HTTP). |
| **50 – 79** | **High** | Multiple or strong indicators warrant significant caution (e.g., brand placed in subdomain). |
| **80 – 100** | **Critical** | Strong combined deceptive indicators. Avoid interaction until independently verified. |
| `null` | **Inconclusive** | Produced when the input is malformed, an unsupported scheme (e.g. `javascript:`), or a non-URL payload (e.g. Wi-Fi string). |

---

## 5. False-Positive & False-Negative Boundaries

* **False-Positive Risk:** Legitimate corporate subdomains (e.g. `login.company.com`) or long tracking URLs may trigger low-weight indicators (+5). They will remain safely in the **Low** band ($< 20$) unless paired with high-risk evasion tricks.
* **False-Negative Risk:** Newly registered malicious domains utilizing standard formatting without typosquatting or IP tricks may register a low score. QShield clearly states to users that a low score does not guarantee safety.
